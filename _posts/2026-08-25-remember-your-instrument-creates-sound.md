## A diary entry from a musician

Admittedly, this is a letter for myself; to remind me when I get distracted (yet) again. Maybe you will find it helpful too.

Remember, your instrument creates sound. You should listen to it.

Remember, your instrument creates sound. And so does everyone else’s, you should listen to them too.

Remember, your instrument creates sound. Seemingly infinite sounds actually, you can explore them if you want.

Remember, your instrument creates sound. What do you want to sound like? You can express yourself.

Remember, your instrument creates sound. Choose the appropriate sound for the context you are in.

Remember, your instrument creates sound. Practice how you want to sound.

Remember, your instrument creates sound. The audience can hear and feel how you sound.
